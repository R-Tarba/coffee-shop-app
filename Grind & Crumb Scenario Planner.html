How the model works and what is fixed
  • Built from the final report. At the recommended settings it reproduces the report: +£285k run-rate, £1,236.5k allocated, £13.5k headroom.
  • Piccadilly: rent saving × 12, less wage and hours cost, plus lunch recovery worth £42.1k at 100% gap closure.
  • Delivery fee: 45,880 small orders a year; the platform takes 30% of the fee; each lost order gives up £0.98 contribution.
  • Dashly: £699k run-rate order value × commission cut; each point of lost volume costs £1.96k.
  • App: £200k gap × recovery × 65% contribution, less £15k running cost.
  • Trinity: lost Trinity profit £70.8k; Briggate's lost profit pool £145.6k × recapture share.
  • New stores: Headingley £58.5k and Kelham £34.6k at forecast, each moving £2.06k per point of sales vs forecast.
  • Waste tracker (+£7k) and the £954k baseline are fixed. The oat trial is a one-off, so it sits in the budget and not in run-rate profit.
  • Planning model, not a forecast. Landlord, platform and lease terms are assumptions until signed.
